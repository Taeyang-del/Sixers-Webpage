Coming Soon

Biking to school is about to get easier. By September 30, Chadwick International will install 125 new bike racks in shaded areas around campus. The Middle and Upper school will gain 68 new spots, with space for 262 bikes overall. Students are reminded to park only in designated areas and keep fire lanes clear. Mr. Fagogenis, our assistant head of school, thinks this new change will make biking to Chadwick even easier and safer for all our community members.
